Cryptography Techniques

1. Caesar Cipher

The Caesar Cipher shifts each letter by a fixed number of positions in the alphabet.

Cipher Key:

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

DEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZABC

Example: DORSU → GRUVX
